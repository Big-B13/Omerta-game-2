THE CLIMB · NEW YORK · 1955

Six families own this city, and one of them owns Havana. You are nobody: a soldier running a racket for a capo who calls you "kid". Take rackets, recruit made men, and climb the family tree until the chair at the head of the table is yours.

Each week you get two moves (three once you are Don). Muscle in on rackets, build, recruit and scheme, then end the week: cash comes in, and the other families make their moves.
Take a racket: click it on the map. Empty ones are cheap; a family's racket means a fight, and fights cost soldiers.
The climb: Soldier → Capo → Underboss → Don. Each step needs more rackets, more cash, more respect.
Win: become the Don, then hold the biggest share of the city (at least 30%) or wipe out every rival family. Lose the crew's loyalty and you're back to nothing.
